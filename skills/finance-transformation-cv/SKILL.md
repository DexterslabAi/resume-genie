# Finance transformation CV

Hiring managers in finance transformation scan for specific vocabulary, systems, scale and outcomes. Generic "led a transformation" bullets don't get shortlisted. This skill adds that domain lens, built from experience of finance transformation and GBS programmes. Read `references/career-tracks.md` (the finance transformation section) and `references/cv-writing.md`.

## What to check and strengthen

1. **Process vocabulary.** Use the exact terms, with acronym and full name once each:
   - P2P / procure-to-pay, O2C / order-to-cash, R2R / record-to-report
   - FP&A, month-end close, intercompany, treasury, tax
2. **Systems.** Name them with modules, for example SAP S/4HANA (FI/CO, MM), Oracle Fusion, Workday Financials, Coupa, Ariba, BlackLine, OneStream, Power BI. "ERP experience" alone carries no weight.
3. **The transformation arc.** Assessment → target operating model → business case → design → build/test → hypercare → benefits realised. Make clear which stages the candidate owned.
4. **Finance KPIs that land:**
   - days to close
   - cost per invoice
   - touchless or straight-through rate
   - first-time-right
   - DPO / DSO
   - working capital released
   - FTE capacity freed
   - audit findings closed
   - SLA attainment
5. **Scale:** countries, legal entities, invoices or transactions a month, programme budget, team or vendor size.
6. **Controls:** SOX, internal controls, segregation of duties, audit readiness.
7. **AI and automation roles:** separate the use case (invoice data extraction, exception triage, cash application matching) from the technology (IDP, RPA, ML, generative AI, agents). Show adoption and control, not just the build.
8. **Product owner and AI pivots:** where it's true, translate programme work into product terms:
   - requirements backlog
   - prioritised releases
   - user acceptance as acceptance criteria
   - adoption KPIs
   - discovery with finance users

## Rules

- Numbers come from the candidate only. Otherwise use `[placeholders]`.
- Never relabel a programme role as a product role. Describe the product-style work that was really done.
