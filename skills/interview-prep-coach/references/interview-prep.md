# Interview prep

STAR stories and likely questions built from real experience.

## Interview prep

### STAR stories (5)
Pick five experiences from the CV that together cover the advert's main themes: delivery, leadership or influence, problem solving, a setback, and the core technical or domain skill.

For each one:
- **Situation:** one or two sentences of context, including the scale.
- **Task:** what the candidate personally owned.
- **Action:** what *they* did, in 2 to 4 concrete steps. Say "I", not "we".
- **Result:** the outcome with a number if the CV has one. Otherwise use a bracketed placeholder like "[Outcome: …]" for the candidate to fill in.
- **Best for:** the question types this story answers.

### Likely questions (10)
Mix the types:
- **behavioural** ("Tell me about a time…"), aimed at the advert's key competencies
- **technical**, about the domain, tools or methods named in the advert
- **situational** ("What would you do if…") about the real job's tensions
- **motivation** (why this role, why this company, why now)
- **gap**: at least one for each significant gap in the analysis. These matter most, because they're the questions candidates dread.

For each one, give a short approach: which story to use, what to emphasise, and what to avoid.

### Questions to ask them
Give three to five sharp questions that show understanding of the role, such as success measures, team setup or the biggest problem in the first 90 days. Avoid questions the advert already answers.
