Procurement Manager - Construction
Meridian Developments, Dubai

About the role
Lead procurement for a pipeline of mixed-use developments, managing strategic sourcing, contracts and a team of buyers.

Responsibilities
- Own strategic sourcing and category management for construction materials and MEP packages
- Run tenders through SAP Ariba and manage contracts end to end
- Lead and develop a team of buyers
- Drive cost savings and supplier performance management (KPIs, SLAs)
- Ensure compliance with procurement policy and UAE regulations

Requirements
- 7+ years in procurement in construction or real estate in the UAE/GCC
- Strategic sourcing and category management
- Contract management and negotiation
- SAP MM and SAP Ariba
- People management
- MCIPS or CIPS qualification

Desirable
- Arabic
- Experience with sustainability or ESG requirements in sourcing
