Senior Product Owner, Finance Automation
Northwind Payments, Dublin (hybrid)

About the role
We are building an AI-assisted platform that automates our finance operations. You will own the product backlog for our Procure-to-Pay and Record-to-Report automation products and work closely with engineering, data science and finance stakeholders.

What you will do
- Own and prioritise the product backlog for P2P and R2R automation
- Translate finance process requirements into clear user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work in an Agile (Scrum) team with engineers and data scientists
- Define KPIs and track adoption, straight-through processing rate and cost per invoice
- Partner with Finance leadership to build the roadmap and business cases
- Drive adoption of AI and machine learning features such as invoice data extraction

What you will bring (required)
- 7+ years in finance operations, finance transformation or shared services
- Experience as a Product Owner or Product Manager in an Agile environment
- Strong knowledge of P2P and R2R processes
- ERP experience, ideally SAP S/4HANA
- Excellent stakeholder management with senior finance leaders

Nice to have
- Experience with AI, intelligent document processing or RPA
- Data skills: SQL, Power BI
- Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) or similar
- Lean Six Sigma
